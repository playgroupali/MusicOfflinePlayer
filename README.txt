INSTALLATION GUIDE (ONLY IOS)
Step 1: Go to deployments and copy the link,
Step 2: Go to Releases and download your color (any color of your choice, this will be your app icon color)
Step 3: Go to https://olibu.github.io/wcg/#/
Step 4: Type in “Offline Music” as the name (You can choose any name but this is the default name)
Step 5: Paste the link inside the URL box.
Step 6: Press OPEN and put in your icon,
Step 7: Press “Ignore Manifest (force no safari ui)”
Step 8: Press Generate, And then Allow.
Step 9: Go to settings, and allow the configuration profile (it will appear as “Downloaded profile” or find it at General>VPN and Device Management)
Step 10: Go inside the app (on your home screen) and press the settings icon and choose your color.
Step 11: Play some MP3S and enjoy it!
